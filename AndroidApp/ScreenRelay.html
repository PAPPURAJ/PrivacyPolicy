Privacy Policy

App: Screen Relay – Mirror, Cast & Control

Developer: Pappuraj Bhottacharjee

Website: https://pappuraj.com

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Summary: Screen Relay works primarily over your local WiFi network. We do not sell your data. We do not require an account. We do not collect personal data for advertising.

1. Overview

Screen Relay allows you to mirror, cast, and control your Android screen in a web browser over the same WiFi network. The app runs a local server on your device to stream screen content to devices you authorize.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Screen Content

When you enable screen sharing, the app uses Android's MediaProjection API to capture your screen and stream it locally to connected devices. This data is transmitted only within your local network.

2.2 Audio (Optional)

If enabled, audio playback capture is streamed locally to the connected browser. Audio is not stored or transmitted to external servers.

2.3 Accessibility Service (Optional)

If you enable remote touch control, Screen Relay uses Android AccessibilityService to inject gestures on your device. This is used strictly to perform actions requested by you from a connected browser session.

The Accessibility Service does NOT:

  • Collect personal data
  • Monitor other apps in the background
  • Transmit data outside your device

2.4 Network Information

The app displays your local IP address and may register a local mDNS hostname (e.g., sr.local) for easier access within your network.

2.5 Connection PIN

A temporary 6-digit PIN is generated for session authentication. This PIN is not stored permanently and is used only during active sharing.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No account information
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics tracking (unless explicitly added in future updates)
  • No personal data sold or shared

4. Data Storage & Retention

Screen Relay does not store screen or audio streams on external servers. Any settings saved remain locally on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

5. Security

All streaming occurs within your local network. However, any device on the same network with the correct URL and PIN may connect while sharing is active.

We recommend:

  • Using trusted WiFi networks only
  • Not sharing the connection URL publicly
  • Stopping sharing when finished

6. Permissions Used

  • Screen Capture (MediaProjection)
  • Accessibility Service (for remote control)
  • Microphone / Audio Capture (optional)
  • Internet & Network State
  • WiFi Multicast
  • Foreground Service & Notifications
All permissions are used strictly to provide app functionality.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since Screen Relay does not collect or store personal data externally, there is generally no stored data to access, modify, or delete.
