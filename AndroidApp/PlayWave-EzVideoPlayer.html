Privacy Policy

PlayWave – Ez Video Player respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information Collection and Use

PlayWave – Ez Video Player does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.

The app accesses video files stored on your device solely to provide offline video playback functionality.

Permissions

The app may request the following permission:

  • Read Media / Storage Access: Required to discover and play video files stored on the user's device.

This permission is used only for core app functionality and not for data collection or sharing.

Data Sharing

PlayWave – Ez Video Player does not share any user data with third parties.

Data Security

All video files remain on the user's device. The app does not upload, backup, or sync any data to external servers.

Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
