Privacy Policy

PlayWave – Ez Video Player is an offline video player application. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how the app handles user data.

Information Collection

PlayWave – Ez Video Player does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.

The app only accesses video files stored locally on your device to enable offline video playback.

Permissions

The app may request the following permission:

  • Media / Storage Access – Required to discover, list, and play video files stored on the user's device.

This permission is used strictly for core app functionality and is never used for tracking, analytics, or data sharing.

Data Sharing

PlayWave – Ez Video Player does not share any user data with third parties.

Data Security

All video files remain on the user's device. The app does not upload, sync, back up, or transmit any data to external servers.

Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
