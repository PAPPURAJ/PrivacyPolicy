Desk Remote – Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 31, 2026

Desk Remote respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Desk Remote Android application handles information when you use the app.

Overview

Desk Remote allows you to control your PC from your phone over a local network. The app is designed to work without user accounts or cloud services.

Information Used by the App

Desk Remote does not collect personal information for its core functionality. The app communicates directly with your PC over your local network to send control commands.

  • Local network discovery: Used to find your PC automatically.
  • Pairing data: A one-time PIN is used to authorize a connection.
  • Control commands: Media and volume control signals sent to your PC.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration is required.
  • No personal identifiers are collected.
  • No contacts, messages, photos, or precise location data are accessed.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Permissions

Desk Remote may request the following permissions depending on enabled features:

  • Network access: To communicate with your PC over Wi-Fi or LAN.
  • Nearby devices / local network: For automatic device discovery.
  • Notifications: To show playback controls on the lock screen or notification panel.

User Control

  • You can unpair your PC at any time.
  • You can disable notifications through Android system settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device.

Children’s Privacy

Desk Remote is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The “Last updated” date will reflect any changes.
