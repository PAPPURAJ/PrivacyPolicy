Summary

Desk Remote is designed to control your PC from your phone over your local network (for example, Wi-Fi). We aim to keep data collection minimal.

Data collection
We do not collect personal data by default
Cloud dependency
No cloud required for core features
Network
Uses your local network to communicate with your PC

What information the app uses

Desk Remote communicates with your computer to send control commands (media, volume, and optionally input features). This communication happens on your local network between your phone and your PC.

  • Device discovery: the app may scan the local network to find your PC automatically.
  • Pairing: the app uses a one-time PIN shown on your PC to pair the devices.
  • Control data: control commands (e.g., play/pause, volume changes) are sent to your PC.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect your contacts, SMS, photos, or precise location for core functionality.

Permissions

Permissions can vary by Android version and enabled features.

  • Network access: required to connect to your PC over Wi-Fi or LAN.
  • Nearby devices / local network: may be used to discover your PC automatically.
  • Notifications: used to show media controls on lock screen / notification shade (optional).

Security

Pairing requires a one-time PIN displayed on your PC. After pairing, the app only controls devices you have authorized. For best safety, use Desk Remote on trusted networks and keep your PC protected with a system password.

User controls

  • Unpair anytime: remove paired devices from the app or desktop extension.
  • Disable discovery: you can turn off auto-discovery and use manual connection (if available).
  • Stop notifications: disable notification controls in Android settings.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app removes the app’s local data from your phone.

Children’s privacy

Desk Remote is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product improvements. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
