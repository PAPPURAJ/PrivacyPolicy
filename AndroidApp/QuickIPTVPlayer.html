Privacy Policy

App Name: Quick IPTV Player
Package: com.pappuraj.quickiptvplayer
Developer: Pappuraj
Effective Date: March 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quick IPTV Player ("the App") handles information. The application is developed and published by Pappuraj.

Important Summary:
Quick IPTV Player does not collect, store, or transmit personal user data. All playlists and settings remain stored locally on the user’s device.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its core functionality, the App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Playlist URLs entered by the user
  • Parsed channel lists from playlists
  • User-selected favorite channels
  • App playback preferences

This information is stored using Android local storage technologies such as DataStore Preferences and remains on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Quick IPTV Player does not collect or process any personal information from users.

  • No user accounts
  • No email addresses collected
  • No phone numbers
  • No device identifiers
  • No location data
  • No analytics tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting services

3. Internet Usage

The App requires internet access only for the following purposes:

  • Loading M3U or M3U8 playlist files from URLs provided by the user
  • Streaming media from channel sources included in those playlists
  • Loading channel logos or images referenced by the playlist

These connections may use HTTP or HTTPS depending on the source provided by the user.

4. Mini Player Feature

Quick IPTV Player includes a mini player that allows users to continue watching or listening to a stream while browsing channels within the app.

The mini player uses the same playback engine as the full-screen player and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

5. No Backend Servers

Quick IPTV Player does not operate a backend service that collects user information. All playlist data, favorites, and settings remain on the user's device.

6. Third-Party Libraries

The App uses standard Android development libraries to support video playback, UI, and data storage. These libraries are used solely to provide application functionality.

Examples include:

  • Android Media3 ExoPlayer
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Coil Image Loader
  • Android DataStore

These libraries are not used by the developer to collect user data.

7. Data Backup

Depending on your Android device settings, the system may include app data in device backups. This behavior is controlled by Android and your device settings.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Content Responsibility

Quick IPTV Player is a media player designed to play user-provided M3U or M3U8 playlists.

The App does not provide, host, distribute, or control any channel content. Users are responsible for the playlists and streaming sources they use within the App.

10. Security

The App is designed to minimize data collection by keeping all user data stored locally. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Choices

You may control your data in the following ways:

  • Remove playlists within the app
  • Clear favorite channels
  • Clear app data through Android settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If changes occur, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
