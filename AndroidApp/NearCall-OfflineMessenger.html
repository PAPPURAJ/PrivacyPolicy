Privacy Policy for NearCall

Last updated: February 10, 2026

NearCall is a peer-to-peer nearby calling and chat application developed by pappuraj.com. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how NearCall handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

NearCall is designed to work without any backend server. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any remote server.

The app may access the following information locally on your device:

  • Display Name – Used to identify you to nearby peers.
  • Chat Messages – Stored locally on your device for chat history.
  • Call Data – Audio and video streams are transmitted directly between devices using peer-to-peer WebRTC.
  • Device & Network Information – Such as local IP address or Bluetooth identifier, used only for nearby device discovery.

2. How Information Is Used

NearCall uses information strictly to provide core functionality:

  • Discover nearby devices using Wi-Fi (LAN) and Bluetooth
  • Enable peer-to-peer audio and video calls
  • Enable real-time text messaging
  • Show online/offline presence and last-seen status
  • Store chat history locally on your device

No data is used for advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling.

3. Data Sharing

NearCall does not share your data with third parties.

  • No cloud servers are involved
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs are used
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared

All communication happens directly between nearby devices.

4. Permissions Explanation

NearCall requests the following permissions only to enable app features:

  • Camera – For video calls
  • Microphone – For audio and video calls
  • Bluetooth – To discover nearby devices
  • Location – Required by Android for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi discovery
  • Network Access – For local peer-to-peer connections
  • Notifications – To notify you of incoming messages or calls

Permissions are used only while the app is running and are never abused.

5. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Android’s internal storage and database mechanisms.

  • Messages and contacts remain on your device
  • You can delete chats or app data at any time
  • Calls use encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer connections

6. Children’s Privacy

NearCall does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not use servers or collect personal data, there is no intentional data collection from minors.

7. Your Control

You have full control over your data:

  • Change your display name at any time
  • Delete chat history from within the app
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
